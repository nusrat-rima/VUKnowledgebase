SECTION 1: INSTITUTION OVERVIEW & HISTORY
================================================================================
1.1 Background & Founding:
* Varendra University (বরেন্দ্র বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়) is the first government-approved 
  private university in Rajshahi and the entire northern region of Bangladesh.
* Founded under the initiative of Mr. Hafizur Rahman Khan (Chairman, Board of 
  Trustees) alongside distinguished academicians, social reformers, and leaders 
  from Rajshahi.
* Established under the Private University Act 2010 to fulfill the longstanding 
  demand for modern, session-jam-free, high-quality tertiary education in North Bengal.

1.2 Approvals & Milestones:
* Ministry of Education Approval: March 14, 2012
* University Grants Commission (UGC) Approval: March 20, 2012
* Academic Classes Commenced: September 24, 2012
* Permanent Establishment Certificate: Awarded by the Ministry of Education 
  and UGC upon fulfilling all statutory conditions, land acquisition, and permanent 
  campus development standards.

1.3 Vision & Mission:
* Vision: To emerge as a center of excellence in higher education, research, and 
  innovation that produces globally competent, ethically grounded, and socially 
  responsible graduates.
* Mission: Providing world-class higher education at an affordable cost, fostering 
  cutting-edge research, and bridging industry-academia partnerships.

1.4 Governance:
* Governed by a Board of Trustees (BoT), Academic Council, Syndicate, and 
  Finance Committee as stipulated by the Private University Act 2010.
* Led by distinguished academics holding key administrative posts (Vice Chancellor, 
  Pro-Vice Chancellor, Treasurer, Registrar, and Deans of Faculties).

SECTION 2: ADMISSION INFORMATION & ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
================================================================================
2.1 Academic Terms / Intake Sessions:
* Varendra University operates primarily on a semester/trimester system:
  - Spring Session : January – April / June
  - Summer Session : May – August
  - Fall Session   : September – December
* Admissions are announced bi-annually or tri-annually depending on department policies.

2.2 Undergraduate Admission Eligibility Criteria:
* General SSC/HSC Requirement:
  - Minimum combined GPA of 5.00 in SSC (or equivalent) and HSC (or equivalent), 
    with not less than GPA 2.50 in either examination.
  - Alternatively, if a student has a GPA of at least 2.00 in either SSC or HSC, 
    their combined total GPA must be at least 6.00 to qualify for eligible humanities 
    and social science programs.
* Program-Specific Science Requirements:
  - B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE): Must have Mathematics and Physics 
    in HSC/equivalent with acceptable grades.
  - B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE): Must have Mathematics and Physics 
    in HSC/equivalent.
  - Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm): Must comply strictly with Pharmacy Council of 
    Bangladesh (PCB) rules. Applicants must pass SSC and HSC in Science group with 
    Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics. Minimum GPA 3.00 in both SSC & HSC 
    (with aggregate GPA 6.50 to 7.00 depending on board circulars), and minimum grade 
    points in Chemistry and Biology.
  - LL.B (Honors): Minimum combined GPA 5.50 across SSC and HSC (minimum 2.50 in each).
* English Medium (GCE O-Level / A-Level):
  - Minimum 5 subjects in O-Level with at least 'C' grades.
  - Minimum 2 subjects in A-Level with at least 'C' grades.
  - For engineering/pharmacy, relevant science subjects (Math, Physics, Chemistry, 
    Biology) must be completed at O and A levels.
* Diploma Engineers:
  - Diploma holders from Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB) in relevant 
    engineering fields (Computer, Electronics, Electrical, etc.) are eligible for 
    admission into B.Sc. Engineering programs with credit transfer/waiver options.
  - Diploma in Pharmacy holders affiliated with PCB can apply for B.Pharm as per regulations.

2.3 Graduate Admission Eligibility Criteria:
* Master of Business Administration (MBA - Regular 2-Year): Bachelor's degree 
  in any discipline with minimum second class or CGPA 2.50.
* Master of Business Administration (Executive MBA / 1-Year): BBA graduates or 
  candidates with bachelor degrees plus relevant executive corporate experience.
* Master of Laws (LL.M): LL.B (Honors) degree from a recognized university.
* M.A. in English: BA (Honors) in English or relevant background.
* MSS in Economics / Sociology / JCMS: Relevant Bachelor degree with acceptable CGPA.
* Master of Public Health (MPH): MBBS, BDS, Nursing, or health sciences degree holders.

2.4 Step-by-Step Admission Procedure:
1. Online Application:
   - Visit the official admission portal: https://vu.edu.bd/ or https://services.vu.edu.bd/
   - Fill in personal, academic, and program preferences.
   - Pay the non-refundable admission application fee online via bKash / bank card.
2. In-Person Application:
   - Prospective students can visit the Admission Information Desk at the Permanent Campus 
     (Rajshahi Bypass Road, Chandrima, Paba) with original transcripts and photocopies.
3. Written Assessment / Viva Voce:
   - Departmental screening tests or interview evaluations are conducted where applicable 
     (especially for Pharmacy, Law, English, and Engineering).
4. Merit List & Final Registration:
   - Selected candidates receive admission clearance SMS/email.
   - Complete document verification and submit the initial admission fee.

2.5 Documents Required for Admission:
* Original and attested photocopies of SSC/O-Level mark sheets and certificates.
* Original and attested photocopies of HSC/A-Level mark sheets and certificates.
* 4 to 6 copies of recent passport-size color photographs.
* Photocopy of National ID Card (NID) of student or Birth Registration Certificate.
* Photocopy of Guardian's / Parent's National ID Card.
* Freedom Fighter Certificate / Tribal Certificate (for quota applicants).

SECTION 5: SCHOLARSHIPS, WAIVERS & FINANCIAL AID POLICIES
================================================================================
Varendra University has one of the most generous waiver frameworks in Bangladesh, 
disbursing millions of Takas annually to meritorious and deserving students.

5.1 Entry Waivers (Based on SSC & HSC Results):
* Golden GPA 5.00 in both SSC & HSC : Up to 100% Tuition Fee Waiver.
* GPA 5.00 in both SSC & HSC        : 50% Tuition Fee Waiver.
* High GPA (e.g. Total GPA 9.5+)     : 25% – 30% Tuition Fee Waiver.
* High GPA in single examination    : Tiered partial waiver (10% - 20%).

5.2 Statutory & Special Category Waivers:
* Freedom Fighter Quota: 100% full tuition waiver for legitimate children of 
  recognized Freedom Fighters (as per UGC statutory quota).
* Siblings / Spouse Waiver: 20% to 25% tuition fee waiver for the second sibling 
  or spouse studying concurrently at Varendra University.
* Female Student Waiver: Dedicated concessions to encourage higher education for 
  female students in Northern Bangladesh.
* Ethnic Minority / Tribal Quota: Special waivers for indigenous students.
* Needy and Meritorious Students: Financial hardship concessions granted via 
  formal application and review by the University Waiver Committee.

5.3 Retention & Semester-Wise Merit Scholarships:
To retain entry waivers or qualify for continuing merit scholarships each semester, 
students must maintain strong academic performance:
* Faculty of Engineering & Science : Minimum SGPA 3.50 in preceding semester.
* Faculty of Business Admin        : Minimum SGPA 3.50 in preceding semester.
* Faculty of Arts & Social Sciences: Minimum SGPA 3.40 in preceding semester.
* Students must take the minimum mandatory credit load per semester without any 
  disciplinary violations.

SECTION 6: PAYMENT TERMS, INSTALLMENT PLANS & PROCEDURES
================================================================================
6.1 Semester Installment Schedule:
Varendra University divides the total payable dues for each semester into structured 
installments to ease the financial burden on students and guardians:
* 1st Installment (At Course Registration / Beginning of Semester):
  - Approximately 35% - 40% of the total semester fee + semester registration fees.
  - Required to complete semester enrollment and access LMS/attendance records.
* 2nd Installment (Before Mid-Term Examination):
  - Approximately 30% - 35% of the total semester fee.
  - Required to obtain Mid-Term Exam clearance and admit card.
* 3rd Installment (Before Final Examination):
  - Remaining 30% of the total semester balance.
  - Required to obtain Final Exam clearance, sit for exams, and view grade sheets.

6.2 Payment Channels & Digital Gateways:
A. Mobile Financial Service - bKash (Integrated Merchant):
   1. Open the bKash App or dial *247#.
   2. Select "Pay Bill" or "Education".
   3. Search and select "Varendra University" from the institution list.
   4. Enter Student ID / Bill Reference Number (from the student's VUPayments slip).
   5. Verify the student name and billing details.
   6. Enter the installment amount and confirm with the bKash PIN.
   7. Save the digital transaction ID and screenshot receipt for records.

B. Online Student Portal (VUPayments):
   - Students can generate digital payment slips by logging into:
     https://services.vu.edu.bd/ or https://vu.edu.bd/
   - Online payments can be processed via SSLCommerz / digital banking gateways using 
     Debit/Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard), Nagad, Rocket, or internet banking.

C. Bank Payments & Cash Deposit Machines (CDM):
   - Payments can be made directly at designated bank branches (e.g., Bank Asia, 
     Dutch-Bangla Bank, Southeast Bank) using VU printed deposit vouchers.
   - Strict University Policy: No cash transactions are accepted directly by campus 
     offices or bus drivers. All payments must be deposited via banking or bKash channels.

6.3 Late Fines & Policy on Overdue Payments:
* Deadlines for each installment are announced in the official semester academic calendar.
* Late payments made after the prescribed deadline may incur a daily or flat late fine.
* Clearing all semester dues is strictly required prior to the issuance of examination 
  admit cards and degree clearance certificates.

SECTION 7: CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE & STUDENT FACILITIES

7.1 Permanent Campus (Paba, Rajshahi):
* Situated on Rajshahi Bypass Road, Chandrima, Paba, Rajshahi.
* A sprawling green campus designed with modern academic buildings, landscaped gardens, 
  water bodies, pedestrian paths, and dedicated administrative wings.
* Designed according to UGC standards with earthquake-resistant architecture, 
  fire safety systems, and automated CCTV surveillance.

7.2 Smart Classrooms:
* Spacious, well-ventilated, and acoustically treated lecture halls.
* Equipped with multimedia projectors, interactive projection screens, sound systems, 
  and high-speed Wi-Fi access.

7.3 Specialized Laboratories (Over 25+ Modern Labs):
* CSE Laboratories: Advanced Software Engineering Labs, AI & Machine Learning Lab, 
  Networking & CISCO Systems Lab, Microprocessor & Digital Electronics Lab.
* EEE Laboratories: Electrical Circuits Lab, Power Systems & High Voltage Lab, 
  Telecommunications & Signal Processing Lab, Electrical Machines Lab.
* Pharmacy Laboratories: Pharmaceutical Microbiology Lab, Phytochemistry & 
  Pharmacognosy Lab, Pharmacology & Animal House, Industrial Pharmacy Simulation Lab, 
  Instrumental Analysis Lab with HPLC and UV-Vis Spectrophotometers.
* Moot Court: Fully functional mock courtroom for Law & Human Rights students to 
  practice advocacy, simulated trials, and judicial procedures.

7.4 Central Library & Digital E-Resource Center:
* Fully automated using the Koha Integrated Library System (ILS).
* Massive repository containing thousands of textbook titles, monographs, reference 
  volumes, national/international journals, and daily periodicals.

  Wiley Online Library, JSTOR, and research databases.
* Separate quiet reading zones, group study cubicles, and computer terminals for 
  digital literature searches.

7.5 Institutional Transport Network (University Buses):

7.6 Cafeteria & Food Services:

  snacks, tea, and beverages for students, faculty, and administrative staff.

7.7 Health, Safety & Utilities:
